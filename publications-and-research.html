Publications and Research

Historical fiction, nonfiction, and original research from The Forgotten History Project.

The Forgotten History Project investigates overlooked historical narratives and recurring patterns that continue shaping the modern world. The publications below explore those questions through historical fiction, nonfiction, and original research.

The Scrolls of Provence

Every surviving history tells two stories: the one that was preserved, and the one that disappeared. The Scrolls of Provence explores what might happen if one forgotten voice from the ancient world returned to challenge what we think we know about the past.

Where the Ladder Ends

The professional ladder isn't broken — the wall it's leaning against is moving. Where the Ladder Ends examines how AI, automation, and structural consolidation are reshaping professional work, and why history suggests this transformation is far larger than a technological revolution.
